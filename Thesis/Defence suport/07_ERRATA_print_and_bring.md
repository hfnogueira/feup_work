**Note on nature of corrections.** Items 1–5 are editorial/bibliographic and affect no result, table, test, or conclusion. Item 6 is a **factual correction to a feature-frequency description**: the RVV rule *counts* (20), MAEs, statistical tests, and conclusions are unchanged, but the claim that LAI is the dominant RVV feature was wrong — LAI appears in 4/20 rules, and heat-stress days and soil water are the dominant RVV features (consistent with the thesis's own Table 4.9 and Appendix E). The support-threshold value used in all computations was 20% throughout; item 4 corrects descriptive text that lagged the code, not the analysis itself.
